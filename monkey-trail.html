MONKEY TRAIL

Explore the Monkey Trail and get a closer look at some of the park's playful monkeys. Enjoy watching them climb, swing, and interact with each other in their natural surroundings.

The Monkey Trail is a fun area for visitors to learn more about monkeys while enjoying a walk through the trees and greenery.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND IT?

The Monkey Trail is located near the forest area and can be reached by following the main walking paths.

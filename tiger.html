TIGERS

The tiger (Panthera tigris) is the largest cat species in the world. Tigers are easily recognised by their orange coat with black stripes, and each tiger has a unique pattern of stripes. They are powerful hunters and mainly feed on animals such as deer, wild boar and other large prey

Tigers are excellent swimmers and can travel long distances in search of food. They are mostly solitary animals and usually live and hunt alone. Tigers can be found in different habitats across Asia, including forests, grasslands and mangrove swamps.
